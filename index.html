A backwards-audio party game for two teams. Someone records a phrase, it gets flipped, the other team copies the gibberish… and then their recording gets flipped back.

Play to
Or play on several phones

The host gets an invite code. Everyone else joins with it on their own phone.

How to play
  1. Speaker (from the team that's not guessing) secretly records a short phrase or song lyric and types it in.
  2. Echo (from the guessing team) hears it backwards and records their best imitation of the gibberish.
  3. Hit Flip it! — the Echo's recording plays backwards, which should sound like the original. The guessing team types their guess.
  4. The game scores it: nailed it = 2 pts, close = 1 pt. Teams alternate.
